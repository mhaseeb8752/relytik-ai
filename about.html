Our Origin Story

Setting the Benchmark in Secondary Market Operations

Founded to solve the operational friction faced by independent ticket brokers, Relytik was established with a single focus: delivering sub-second queue execution, flawless carting, and dedicated 24/7 virtual assistance.

As secondary ticketing platforms evolved into complex, high-frequency ecosystems, brokerages faced severe operational bottlenecks—from missed presale drops to delayed barcode fulfillment. Relytik bridged this gap by assembling a world-class team of live event analysts, operational specialists, and software engineers.

Today, Relytik serves over 350 independent ticket brokers, agencies, and enterprise resellers across North America and Europe, processing over $180M in secondary market inventory value with an industry-leading 99.9% execution SLA.
